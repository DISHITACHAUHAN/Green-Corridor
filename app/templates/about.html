About Green Corridor AI Platform

National Emergency Organ Transportation Network — Design, Protocols & Architecture
System Mission & Vision

The Green Corridor AI Platform is an advanced multi-stakeholder emergency logistics system engineered to eliminate transit bottlenecks in cadaveric and living organ donations. Operating across municipal, state, and national traffic frameworks, the platform connects:

  • Donor Harvesting Hospitals: Instant notification of cross-clamp completion, organ packing, and IoT box synchronization.
  • Ambulance Fleet Control: Real-time telemetric tracking, speed telemetry, and route guidance via OSRM engines.
  • City Traffic Police: Dynamic green-wave signal clearance and convoy escort deployment along designated corridors.
  • Recipient Transplant Theatres: Live countdown timers ensuring recipient preparation coincides with ambulance arrival.
